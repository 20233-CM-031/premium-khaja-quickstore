# SOUQONE QUICKSTORE

## ARTIFICIAL JEWELLERY EDITION — MASTER BUILD PROMPT

You are building a production-grade **SouqOne QuickStore** for an Artificial Jewellery / Fashion Jewellery brand.

This is NOT a conventional website.

This is NOT a Shopify clone.

This is NOT merely an online product catalogue.

Build it as a new type of digital commerce system called a:

# QUICKSTORE

A QuickStore is:

**Storefront + Customer Intelligence + Marketing Engine + Commerce + Membership + Retention**

The customer-facing store is only one layer.

The system underneath must help the jewellery business:

* attract customers
* identify customers
* understand customer behaviour
* showcase products
* create desire
* increase basket size
* convert visitors
* capture customer information
* build membership
* generate repeat purchases
* encourage referrals
* track marketing
* understand product performance
* automate customer engagement

The final experience should make the user think:

> "This is the brand's digital store."

not:

> "This is a website."

# 2. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The central principle is:

> Artificial Jewellery is often purchased because the customer sees a look they want to wear.

Therefore the product experience must answer:

* What does it look like?
* How would I style it?
* What occasion is it suitable for?
* What can I wear with it?
* What else matches it?
* Is it currently available?
* What is the price?
* Is there an offer?
* Is it worth adding another item?

Do not make the store feel like an inventory database.

Make it feel like a:

# DIGITAL JEWELLERY SHOWROOM
